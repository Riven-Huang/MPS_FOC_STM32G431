DESCRIPTION                                                              FEATURES
The MP6539B is a gate driver IC designed for                                  Supports 100V Operation
three-phase, brushless DC motor driver                                        120V VBST Maximum Voltage
applications. The MP6539B is capable of                                       Integrated Current-Sense Amplifier
driving three half-bridges consisting of six N-                               Low-Power Sleep Mode for Battery-
channel power MOSFETs up to 100V.                                              Powered Applications
The MP6539B uses a bootstrap capacitor to                                     Adjustable Dead-Time Control to Prevent
generate a supply voltage for the high-side                                    Shoot-Through
MOSFET driver. An internal charge pump                                        Thermal Shutdown and Under-Voltage
maintains the high-side gate drive if the output                               Lockout (UVLO) Protection
is held high for an extended period of time.                                  Fault Indication Output
                                                                              Available in Thermally Enhanced, Surface-
Internal safety features include shoot-through
                                                                               Mounted TSSOP and QFN Packages
protection, adjustable dead-time control, under-
voltage lockout (UVLO), and thermal shutdown.                            APPLICATIONS
The MP6539B is similar to the MP6539. It does                                 Three-Phase, Brushless DC Motors and
not implement auto bootstrap charging, does                                    Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors
not include over-current protection, and is                                   Power Drills
powered only from the VDD pin (VIN and LDO                                    E-Bike
have been removed).
